# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person using a laptop while writing messages, notes, or documents. They need to turn speech into text quickly when typing is tiring or would lead to spelling mistakes.

## Product Purpose

A private, local push-to-talk dictation tool. The user holds a shortcut, speaks naturally in French, English, Arabic, or Turkish, releases it, and receives a transcription pasted directly into the focused field. Success means the interaction feels faster and less effortful than typing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, professional, and cool. The interface should feel dependable and focused, giving the user confidence that it is listening, processing, and ready without demanding attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid a cluttered dashboard, gamer aesthetics, noisy decoration, and settings-heavy workflows. Never make the user hunt for the one action they need: speak, then continue writing.

## Design Principles

1. Make dictation immediate: the primary workflow must take no more than holding and releasing a shortcut.
2. Make state unmistakable: ready, listening, transcribing, and pasted must be recognisable at a glance.
3. Protect writing flow: paste automatically and keep configuration out of the way.
4. Keep the core private and fast: transcription runs locally, with no mandatory network service.
5. Earn extra intelligence: grammar correction is an optional future enhancement only if it preserves a fast experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong text contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, clear non-colour state cues, and restrained motion that respects the system's reduced-motion preference. Support the user's four spoken languages through automatic language detection.
